Polyhedron brings translation editing, search, review, and export into one workspace for [b]Baldur’s Gate 3, Divinity: Original Sin, and Divinity: Original Sin 2[/b]. Spend less time managing files and more time translating.

[b]This is a translation tool, not a finished language pack.[/b]

[size=4][color=#A7F175][b]WHAT YOU CAN DO[/b][/color][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Translate & review[/b] — side-by-side or stacked editing, supported gender variants, entry history, review statuses, and formatting-tag helpers.[/*]
[*][b]Find what matters[/b] — precise search, find & replace, speaker/status filters, repeated-string detection, and content-ID display and copying.[/*]
[*][b]Keep wording consistent[/b] — compare different translations of identical strings, apply preferred wording, and maintain a glossary.[/*]
[*][b]Understand the context[/b] — BG3 dialogue references, game data, spells, actions, conditions, and a creature guide with linked translations.[/*]
[*][b]Use optional translation providers[/b] — Google Translate, DeepL, Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, or Grok; translate individual entries or batches with configurable AI prompts.[/*]
[*][b]Export & manage files[/b] — XML export, plus supported BG3 PAK/ZIP packaging, extraction, merging, and localization injection.[/*]
[*][b]Back up & continue elsewhere[/b] — portable .pws workspaces, optional Google Drive synchronization, and a browser/mobile companion for supported editing views.[/*]
[*][b]Make it yours[/b] — a dark interface with a customizable accent, button text color, and application-icon styles.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Game support:[/b] BG3 supports XML/PAK/ZIP workflows and dedicated reference tools. Divinity profiles support localization XML editing and export. Packaging, extraction, and injection remain desktop features.

[b]Online translation is optional[/b] and requires your own credentials where applicable. Provider charges are separate; always review generated text before using it in-game.

[size=4][color=#A7F175][b]GET STARTED[/b][/color][/size]

[list=1]
[*]Download the appropriate version from [b]Files[/b]. On Windows, run the installer. On macOS, open the DMG and drag [b]Polyhedron.app[/b] into [b]Applications[/b].[/*]
[*]Launch Polyhedron, choose your game and language pair, then create or select a project.[/*]
[*]Open your localization file and start translating. To restore an existing workspace, use [b]Workspace → Import Workspace[/b].[/*]
[/list]

Polyhedron is a standalone application: [b]do not install it through Vortex or place it in the game’s Mods folder[/b]. You do not need the game installed just to edit a localization file.

[b]Before BG3 injection:[/b] close the game and keep your own project and game-file backups. Injection modifies the game’s localization files; updates or file verification may restore the originals.

[b]Workspace import replaces your current workspace; it does not merge it.[/b] Local editing requires no Google account. Drive synchronization and online providers require an internet connection.
